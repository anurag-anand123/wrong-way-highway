Wrong Way Highway · iOS beta

The highway is open for testing.

App Store pre-orders aren’t open yet. In the meantime, you can try the beta of Wrong Way Highway on your iPhone or iPad through TestFlight.

Join the TestFlight beta ↗

Open this page on the iPhone or iPad you want to play on. TestFlight will show device requirements and whether a beta build is available.

Three steps to your first run.

  1. Install TestFlight.

    Get Apple’s TestFlight app from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. Open the beta invitation.

    Return here and tap Join the TestFlight beta. Follow the invitation into TestFlight.

  3. Accept, install, and play.

    Accept the invitation if prompted, then choose Install. Once it finishes, open Wrong Way Highway and start your first run.

Help shape the game.

This is a beta version, so you may encounter bugs or changes as development continues. Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight to share your experience, or email me.

If the invitation says the beta is full, unavailable, or incompatible with your device, follow the information shown in TestFlight and check back later.

For more help, see Apple’s TestFlight instructions.
