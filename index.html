Before you play

A few things to know.

When does Wrong Way Highway launch?

The planned launch date is October 26, 2026. Check the store listing for availability on your device.

Can I play on iPhone or iPad now?

You can try the beta through TestFlight. App Store pre-orders are not open yet. See the beta installation steps.

How do I pre-register on Android?

Open the Google Play listing on your device and follow the pre-registration instructions shown there.

Does the game include ads?

The game uses Google AdMob to display ads. You can read more in the privacy policy.

How can I send feedback?

Use TestFlight’s feedback option for the iOS beta, or email Anurag with a description of what happened and your device model.
