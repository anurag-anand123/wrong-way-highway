Wrong Way Highway is a mobile driving game about heading into oncoming traffic and keeping your run alive. Dodge vehicles, collect power-ups, and survive increasingly difficult waves.

Anurag Anand is the solo developer and founder of Enteriosoft. His earlier game, Extreme Balancer 3, has reached more than 50 million downloads.
